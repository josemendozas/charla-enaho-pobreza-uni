# Papers y recursos sugeridos

## Metodología: DiD con adopción escalonada

- **Callaway, B. & Sant'Anna, P.H.C. (2021).** "Difference-in-Differences with Multiple Time Periods." *Journal of Econometrics*, 225(2), 200-230.
- **Goodman-Bacon, A. (2021).** "Difference-in-Differences with Variation in Treatment Timing." *Journal of Econometrics*, 225(2), 254-277.
- **de Chaisemartin, C. & D'Haultfœuille, X. (2020).** "Two-Way Fixed Effects Estimators with Heterogeneous Treatment Effects." *American Economic Review*, 110(9), 2964-2996.
- **Roth, J. et al. (2023).** "What's Trending in Difference-in-Differences? A Synthesis of the Recent Econometrics Literature." *Journal of Econometrics*, 235(2), 2218-2244.

## Libros de métodos cuasiexperimentales

- **Angrist, J. & Pischke, J.S.** *Mostly Harmless Econometrics*. Princeton University Press, 2009.
- **Cunningham, S.** *Causal Inference: The Mixtape*. Yale University Press, 2021. [Gratuito online](https://mixtape.scunning.com/)
- **Huntington-Klein, N.** *The Effect: An Introduction to Research Design and Causality*. CRC Press, 2021. [Gratuito online](https://theeffectbook.net/)

## Papers aplicados con ENAHO (ejemplos de diseño)

- **Mendoza, J. (2026).** "Banking Agents and Private Remittances in Rural Peru." *Economics Bulletin* (bajo revisión). — DiD escalonado, SBS + ENAHO.
- **Escobal, J. & Torero, M. (2005).** "Adverse Geography and Differences in Welfare in Peru." En *Spatial Inequality and Development*, Oxford. — Geografía como instrumento.
- **Alcázar, L. (2012).** "El gasto público social frente a la infancia." GRADE. — ENAHO + SIAF.
- **Yamada, G. & Castro, J.F. (2012).** "Poverty, Inequality and Social Policies in Peru." PUCP Working Paper. — Descomposiciones con ENAHO.
- **Gertler, P., Martinez, S. & Rubio-Codina, M. (2012).** "Investing Cash Transfers to Raise Long-Term Living Standards." *AEJ: Applied Economics*. — Evaluación de Juntos con diseño cuasiexperimental.

## Papers sobre inclusión financiera y desarrollo

- **Burgess, R. & Pande, R. (2005).** "Do Rural Banks Matter?" *American Economic Review*, 95(3), 780-795.
- **Jack, W. & Suri, T. (2014).** "Risk Sharing and Transactions Costs." *American Economic Review*, 104(1), 183-223.
- **Fonseca, J. & Matray, A. (2024).** "The Real Effects of Banking the Poor: Evidence from Brazil." *Journal of Financial Economics*, 152.
